I'm Junho Bae, an AI Research Engineer at SK AX. I design and build agents
and the retrieval systems behind them, and I've been doing it in production
since 2024.

The work I'm proudest of is a customer-support agent for a telecom carrier.
I owned the retrieval side — a domain embedding model trained with
hard-negative mining, hybrid sparse–dense search, and an LLM reranker that
picks up new domain terms without retraining — and ran the service from beta
to general availability. Right now I lead the vector and agent side of an
AI-ready data platform: turning documents, tables and databases into
asset-level context that multi-agent systems can search through MCP.

Before this I was an analytics engineer at Mobigen, and before that a
master's student in information systems at Yonsei, where I worked on graph
neural networks for thin-file credit scoring and co-authored a couple of
papers.

I write here when something is worth keeping. Posts published elsewhere —
including on SK's DEVOCEAN — are collected on the [writing](/writing/) page.
